QuoteClip

Find the YouTube clip your quote came from. Type a line you remember, get the video and the exact timestamp.

About

QuoteClip identifies the source of a quote — a movie, TV show, speech, song, or viral video — and finds a YouTube clip where the line is actually spoken. The app uses Google's Gemini and the YouTube Data API to identify candidate videos and verify the audio.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data QuoteClip collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Anonymous account ID. When you first open QuoteClip, an anonymous account is created via Firebase Authentication. This lets us store your search history and saved clips across sessions without requiring sign-in. No personal information is associated with this anonymous ID.
  • Sign-in information (optional). If you choose to sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we store your email address, display name, profile photo URL, and the sign-in providers you've linked. This is used to sync your data across devices.
  • Search history. The quotes you search for are stored in your account so they can appear as recents in the app. You can disable history recording or delete entries from within the app.
  • Saved items and lists. Clips you save (and any lists you create) are stored in your account.
  • Search queries sent to Google. The quotes you search are sent to Google's Gemini API and the YouTube Data API to identify the source and locate clips. These are processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy.

What We Don't Collect

  • We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, or precise location.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising.
  • We do not track your activity outside the app.

Third-Party Services

QuoteClip uses the following Google services:

  • Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check) — for accounts, data storage, and backend processing.
  • Gemini API — to identify the source of a quote and verify clips.
  • YouTube Data API Services — to find candidate videos.

YouTube API Services

QuoteClip uses YouTube API Services. By using QuoteClip, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete individual saved clips, lists, and search history entries from within the app.
  • Disable search history recording in app settings.
  • Request full account deletion by emailing the address below.

Children's Privacy

QuoteClip is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
