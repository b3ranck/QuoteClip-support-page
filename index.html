quoteclip

find the clip your quote came from. type a line you remember, get the video and the exact timestamp.

about

what this is

QuoteClip identifies the source of a quote — a movie, TV show, speech, song, or viral video — and finds a clip where the line is actually spoken. The app uses Google's Gemini and the YouTube Data API to identify candidate videos and verify the audio.

privacy policy

what we collect, what we don't

last updated · june 10, 2026

information we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. When you first open QuoteClip, an anonymous account is created via Firebase Authentication so we can store your search history and saved clips across sessions without requiring sign-in. No personal information is associated with this anonymous ID.
  • Sign-in information (optional). If you choose to sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we store your email address, display name, profile photo URL, and the sign-in providers you've linked. This is used to sync your data across devices.
  • Search history. The quotes you search for are stored in your account so they can appear as recents in the app. You can disable history recording or delete entries from within the app.
  • Saved items and lists. Clips you save (and any lists you create) are stored in your account.
  • Search queries sent to Google's AI. The quotes you search (and any source you add) are sent to Google's Gemini API — a third-party AI service — and the YouTube Data API to identify the source and locate clips. See AI processing & your consent below.

what we don't collect

  • We don't collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, or precise location.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties for advertising.
  • We don't track your activity outside the app.

third-party services

QuoteClip uses the following Google services:

  • Firebase — Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check (accounts, data storage, backend processing).
  • Gemini API — Google's AI model, used to identify the source of a quote and verify clips.
  • YouTube Data API Services — to find candidate videos.

YouTube API Services notice. QuoteClip uses YouTube API Services. By using QuoteClip, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

AI processing & your consent

To find your clip, the quote you type or speak — and any source you add — is sent to Google's Gemini API, a third-party AI service, along with the YouTube Data API. We ask for your explicit consent in the app, on the welcome screen shown before your first search, and only send this data after you agree.

Google processes this data solely as our service provider, under Google's Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms, which provide a level of data protection equivalent to this policy. Because QuoteClip uses the paid Gemini API tier, Google does not use your searches to train its models. We never sell your searches or use them for advertising. You can withdraw consent at any time — see your rights below.

your rights

  • Delete individual saved clips, lists, and search history entries from within the app.
  • Disable search history recording in app settings.
  • Withdraw consent for AI processing. Your searches are only sent to Google's Gemini AI when you run a search, so you can stop sharing at any time by simply not searching. You can also delete your stored searches or your entire account, as described here.
  • Delete your account and all associated data directly from the app. Open the Profile tab and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account, saved clips, lists, and search history — the deletion can't be undone.
  • If you can't reach the in-app delete option, you can request account deletion by emailing the address below.

children's privacy

QuoteClip is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
